# Feedback to BAFU

This page shows what was reported to BAFU, what BAFU answered, and what is resolved. Feedback to BAFU is the main purpose of this workspace.

BAFU, the Swiss Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN), publishes the BAFU/UVEK life-cycle inventory database and decides what it changes. Nothing on this site promises that BAFU will change its data.

## How to read this page

- **Reported to BAFU.** A finding is shown as reported when its record holds the date of the report and a public link that shows it. Where a record holds no such date, the page says "not recorded".
- **BAFU's answer.** An answer is shown as a short summary, with its date and a link to the place where it is recorded. The summary is attributed to BAFU as an office. It names no person and quotes no message. An answer says what BAFU said. It does not show that the data has changed.
- **Resolved.** A finding is resolved once a BAFU release or file that the record names covers the point, whether or not that followed a report made here.
- **Repeated, not yet reported.** A second person repeated the measurement and got the same result. The finding has not been reported to BAFU yet.

## A community patch is not BAFU's correction

Some findings have a community patch. A community patch changes a copy of BAFU's release for users who need the change now. It is made and authorised within this community, not by BAFU, and it does not change the status of a finding. The [patched copy](patch-and-build.md) page lists the patches.

## Where the evidence is

Each finding has its own page. The page says what was observed and where the finding is discussed. The [findings](findings.md) page lists them all, and [take part](take-part.md) explains how to repeat a measurement.
